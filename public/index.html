What this does

It joins the datasets you used to open one by one.

The lake search starts with Ontario Aquatic Resource Area and Waterbody Location Identifier records, then joins stocking. Once you select a lake, it checks official fishing access, Ontario Road Network context, MNR road barriers, MNR unpatented-land and CLUPA context, Ontario active fires and Environment Canada SWOB observations.

  • Missing data stays missing; no guessed lake/access/condition records.
  • Distance-from-you uses straight-line distance only, not drive time.
  • Crown-land information is planning/mapping context, not legal permission to camp or cross property.
  • Always verify current fishing regulations and emergency/fire information.
Score contract

Useful scores, narrow claims.

Lake Match measures fit to your fish/lake filters and the source evidence available. Trip Context combines Lake Match with mapped access, road/barrier proximity, active-fire proximity and the nearest recent Environment Canada station observation.

Neither number is a catch forecast, fish-population estimate, legal access determination, route-time estimate or safety clearance.
